Which services do you offer?

I offer WordPress development (custom websites, Elementor, WooCommerce stores, landing pages, speed optimisation, maintenance, redesigns, custom plugins and integrations) and digital marketing (SEO, Google and Meta ads, social media, content, email and WhatsApp marketing, and analytics). You can hire me for one service or for the whole journey.

Do you build websites on WordPress only?

WordPress is my specialty, and for most business websites it is the right choice: it is flexible, easy to manage and good for SEO. If a project clearly needs something else, I will tell you honestly rather than force a fit.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. I build WooCommerce stores with product setup, payment gateways, shipping and tax settings, and a simple checkout. I can also connect the store to your marketing so you know which campaigns bring orders.

Can you fix or redesign my existing website?

Yes. I start by reviewing your current design, speed, SEO and code, then suggest whether a fix, a partial rebuild or a full redesign makes sense. I protect your existing content and search rankings during any move.

Will my website be fast and SEO-friendly?

That is how I build every site: clean code, optimised images, caching, mobile-first layouts, proper heading structure, meta tags and schema. It gives your content and marketing the best possible start in Google.

How much does a website or marketing service cost?

It depends on the pages, features and goals. After a short conversation I send a written quote that lists exactly what is included. Marketing services are usually a monthly retainer or a one-time project fee, plus your own ad budget where ads are involved.

How long will my website take?

A typical business website takes a few weeks, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content and feedback come in. Your quote includes a timeline so you know what to expect at each stage.

How soon will SEO or ads show results?

Ads can bring visitors within days of launching, while SEO is a longer-term investment that builds over months. I set realistic expectations at the start and share monthly reports so you can see progress. I never promise a ranking.

Will I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your website, content, domain and marketing accounts (such as Google Analytics and Google Ads) stay in your name. I get access to work on them, and you can take them back at any time.

Do you work with clients outside my city or country?

Yes. I work remotely through calls, chat and shared documents, and plan meeting times around your time zone.

Do you offer support after the website launches?

Yes. I offer monthly care plans that cover updates, backups, security checks, uptime monitoring and small changes, so your website keeps running smoothly while you focus on your business.

Can you manage my hosting and domain?

Yes. I can help you choose reliable hosting, set up SSL and business email, and manage your domain, or work with the provider you already use.
